# Art Styles and Use Case Categories for Model Selection

This document provides a structured taxonomy of art styles and use case categories derived from industry research. Use this guide to direct model selection based on user requirements.

## Executive Summary

Image generation use cases fall into two primary domains:
- **Professional/Commercial** (prioritize photorealism, accuracy, trust-building)
- **Personal/Creative** (prioritize expression, experimentation, trending aesthetics)

Model selection should consider:
1. **Quality requirements** (photorealistic vs. stylized)
2. **Material accuracy** (product photography vs. concept art)
3. **Speed vs. quality trade-offs** (social media vs. marketing campaigns)
4. **Consistency needs** (brand materials vs. one-off creations)

---

## Use Case Categories

### 1. Marketing & Advertising

**Primary Requirements:**
- Photorealistic product shots
- Brand consistency
- Rapid A/B testing capability
- Platform-specific optimization

**Art Styles:**
- **Photorealistic product shots** (85% material accuracy for complex surfaces)
- **Lifestyle imagery** (aspirational settings, emotional connection)
- **Lo-fi and vintage aesthetics** (warm tones, film grain, nostalgic effects)
- **Weird angles** (unconventional perspectives for visual distinctiveness)
- **Platform-specific styles:**
  - Instagram: Highly saturated, bold colors, aspirational
  - LinkedIn: Professional minimalist, muted corporate palettes
  - TikTok: Raw, authentic-looking (even when AI-generated)

**Model Selection Criteria:**
- High photorealism capability
- Fine material control (sequins, holographic, iridescent)
- Batch generation support
- Aspect ratio flexibility

**Recommended Models:**
- Primary: Flux Pro (photorealism, brand accuracy)
- Secondary: Stable Diffusion 3.5 (cost-effective batch generation)
- Specialized: Midjourney (lifestyle imagery, aspirational aesthetics)

## Consistency Requirements

### High Consistency Needs (Brand Materials, Product Lines, Character Design)
- **Recommended**: Flux Pro (fine parameter control)
- **Alternative**: Stable Diffusion + ControlNet (character consistency)
- **Strategy**: Use reference images, seed control, parameter locking

### Medium Consistency Needs (Campaign Variations, Style Cohesion)
- **Recommended**: Midjourney (style consistency within parameters)
- **Alternative**: Flux models (style reference features)
- **Strategy**: Use style references, variation parameters

### Low Consistency Needs (Exploration, Social Content, Experimentation)
- **Recommended**: Any model (optimize for speed/cost)
- **Strategy**: Maximize variation, trend responsiveness

## Cost Optimization Strategies

### High-Volume Commercial Use
1. **API Integration**: Stable Diffusion 3.5 (self-hosted or API)
2. **Batch Processing**: Flux models with programmatic generation
3. **Hybrid Approach**: Midjourney for hero images, SD for variations

### Small Business / Limited Budget
1. **Free Tier**: Stable Diffusion (self-hosted)
2. **Pay-as-you-go**: Imagen 4 or Flux models
3. **Subscription**: Midjourney Basic ($8/month)

### Enterprise Scale
1. **Licensed Models**: Stable Diffusion commercial license
2. **API Services**: Replicate, Together AI (usage-based)
3. **Custom Fine-tuning**: Stable Diffusion or Flux (brand-specific)

1. **Use case requirements** (photorealism vs. artistic)

The wyrd-gen catalog should enable users to discover the right model for their specific needs through structured guidance based on these categories.
